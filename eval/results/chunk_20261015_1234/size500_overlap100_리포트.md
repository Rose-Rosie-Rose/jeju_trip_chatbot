### 4 △ · -
- 질문: 돔베가 뭐야?
- 정답: Cutting board
- 챗봇 답변: Dombae-gogi (돔베고기) refers to a traditional dish made from Jeju black pork. The term "dombae" means "cutting board" in the Jeju dialect, and the dish is served on a cutting board to enjoy the juicy flavors of the meat. It is sliced immediately before serving to retain its moisture and is typically enjoyed with a dipping sauce made from fermented anchovy sauce mixed with soju.
